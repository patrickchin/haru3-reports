## Slide 3 — Why It Matters

Site supervisors lose **3–8 hours per week per manager** to manual
report writing (source: original deck slide 11, founder data — needs
external citation, e.g., McKinsey *Reinventing Construction*, 2017).

When reports are late or missing, three things happen:

- Critical site details are forgotten before they're written down.
- Owners and PMs lose visibility into progress.
- Risk and incident data never make it into the audit trail.

Harpa Pro replaces that workflow with a 60-second voice note.

**Speaker notes:** Use a real, citable statistic. Replace the broken
stacked-percentage chart from the original slide 19. One number, one
source.

## What changed vs the original 28-slide deck

| Original | New | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 28 slides | 10 slides + appendix | YC: "10 slides is enough." |
| TOC + 5 dividers | none | YC decks don't have TOCs or dividers. |
| Two products (procurement + voice) | One (voice site reports) | Pick one. The 90%-built prototype is voice. |
| Empty "Add App screenshots here" | 3 real prototype screenshots | The screenshots exist on slide 15. |
| Fictional B2C couple as testimonial | Real PM quote | Wrong customer on the original slide 20. |
| No market sizing | Bottoms-up SOM + TAM | Original deck had none. |
| Stock-photo pricing screenshot | Real prices, ACV, margin | Show your numbers. |
| Advisor + empty box on team slide | 2 founders only, advisor in appendix | YC: advisors don't belong on the team slide. |
| "Only seeking a single Angel" | "$200K SAFE @ $X cap, 18mo runway, [milestones]" | Standard ask format. |
| Date watermark on every slide | Cover only | Distracting; original deck had inconsistent dates (2023 vs 2025). |
